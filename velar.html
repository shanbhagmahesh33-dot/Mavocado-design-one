01 / Project at a Glance

One rental journey.
Both sides, considered.

An independent premium car rental platform concept with six models and five sample India branches. The customer website and admin workspace share the same browser-local inventory, booking records and pricing rules in the demo.

Industry
Premium vehicle rentals · India / INR
Platform
Customer website & operations panel · Desktop & mobile
Scope
UX/UI, visual identity, motion & interactive development
Focus
OTP flow, date-based inventory, checkout & branch operations
02 / The Design Challenge

Make booking feel effortless.
Make operations feel connected.

A premium website needs more than desirable cars. Customers need clear prices and a confident next step; branch teams need to understand stock, bookings and returns without losing context.

Help customers choose with confidence

Consistently scaled cars, category filters and icon-led specifications help customers compare models. Colour and powertrain choices update the illustrative price.

Connect the customer and branch view

A checkout hold appears in operations, alongside its vehicle, branch, configuration, dates, customer details and status. Staff can follow a booking through pickup and return.

Make stock understandable

Quantity is derived from individually registered vehicles. Maintenance, open bookings and turnaround time affect date-based availability instead of relying on a single stock number.

03 / The Experience We Built

From the first sign-in
to the last return.

Four connected views show the customer journey and the operational work behind it. The preview demonstrates realistic interactions while keeping OTP, payments, messages and calls explicitly simulated.

Website discovery

A different class of drive.

A confident car-led landing page leads into branch and date/time search. Categories, configuration choices and useful specifications support a clear decision.

Mobile number & OTP preview

A simple start. A clearer next step.

A dedicated access experience introduces mobile sign-in before the website. The local one-time code, expiry and retry states demonstrate the intended interaction without sending an SMS.

Checkout & recovery

One last step. Then it’s yours.

A transparent order summary carries the selected car and rental dates into test checkout. Customers can explore payment methods, failed-payment recovery and a successful demo confirmation.

Fleet, bookings & returns

Every journey, in view.

The admin workspace manages individually registered cars, quantities and branch rates. Staff can inspect bookings, export records, preview notifications, simulate a return call and complete a rental.

04 / Decisions Behind the Design

Clear choices for customers.
Clear control for the team.

The platform separates presentation from inventory rules, preserves decisions through checkout and makes exceptional states understandable.

Every vehicle has its own record

Bulk entry adds separate registrations for each car. Operational state controls protect cars with open bookings, keeping the presentation grounded in physical inventory.

Keep prices visible and stable

Weekday, weekend, configuration and deposit amounts are itemised before checkout. Editing a branch rate affects new quotes while preserving an existing booking snapshot.

Design the recovery path too

A temporary hold creates room for checkout. The payment preview includes unsuccessful attempts, retry, confirmation and a downloadable demo receipt.

Show the limits honestly

The local code does not verify phone ownership. Payment, WhatsApp and reminder-call screens explicitly show simulation states. The prototype makes no claim of live transactions or customer delivery.

05 / The Design Language

One identity.
Two complementary workspaces.

The automotive website stays expressive; the admin workspace stays calm and practical. Shared typography, restrained colours and consistent controls make the two experiences feel related.

A distinctive VELAR identity

An angular mark and spaced wordmark give the brand an automotive character without competing with the cars or operational details.

Typography for browsing and doing

DM Sans combines short, oversized website headings with compact labels, practical tables and aligned information in the admin panel.

A consistent visual rhythm

Square actions, fine dividers, muted green panels and clear status treatments connect the customer website to the operations workspace.

06 / What the Project Delivered

More than a landing page.
A connected platform prototype.

The delivery connects a responsive rental website to an operations application, with working browser-local flows and a separately tested Node/PostgreSQL booking foundation. Production provider connections remain a later step.

Customer website & mobile access

Premium car discovery, branch and date/time search, colour and fuel configuration, mobile-number entry and a single-use local demo-code flow.

Checkout & booking states

Temporary holds, Card / UPI / Net Banking test choices, successful and failed payment states, retry, booking confirmation and a demo receipt.

Fleet & branch operations

Owner and manager preview roles, individual vehicles and quantities, rentable/offline states, branch pricing, maintenance, booking filters and scoped CSV exports.

Communication & technical foundation

Customer and branch WhatsApp previews, two-hour return-call simulation, pickup and return actions. The underlying repository includes server-priced PostgreSQL holds and 63 passing automated tests.

Interactive prototype. All customer details, branch locations and rates are illustrative. OTP, payments, messages and calls are simulations; no money is charged and no customer is contacted. Mockup visuals are presentation renders of the project screens.
