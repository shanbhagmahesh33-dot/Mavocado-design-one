GOOD TO KNOW

Here for your questions.

A little information can make a big difference.

How can I request an appointment?+

Choose “Book an appointment”, select a specialty and a preferred date. This design preview demonstrates the request journey; it does not create a real appointment.

What should I bring for my first visit?+

Keep your identification, relevant previous reports and a list of current medicines together. Confirm any specific preparation directly with your care provider.

How do I choose a specialty?+

Explore the specialties above to understand the areas of care. If you are unsure, ask a qualified care team for guidance instead of trying to diagnose yourself.

Can I check insurance coverage before a visit?+

Yes—ask your insurer and the hospital about eligibility, exclusions and any pre-authorisation requirements before making a decision.
