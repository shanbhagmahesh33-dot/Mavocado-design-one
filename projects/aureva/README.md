# Auréva — Skin & Beauty Atelier

A responsive, buildless single-page website concept. Upload the contents of `dist/` to any static host or GitHub Pages.

## Included
- Custom generated transparent logo and original photographic assets for the hero, six services, introduction, atelier, CTA banner and blended footer.
- White background, compact glass header, responsive navigation.
- Filterable service collection; keyboard-accessible service details dialogs.
- Consultation-led laser and anti-ageing content; FAQ accordions; scroll reveals; pause and reduced-motion controls.
- Browser-local appointment request preparation with copy action. No data is transmitted or persisted; no real booking is confirmed.

## Before a real business launch
Replace the working brand, verified service availability, practitioner qualifications, prices, address, opening times, booking policies, privacy terms and real booking destination. Generated images are illustrative; they are not records of real patients, staff, facilities or results. Do not present them as treatment evidence. Review medical-service copy with the actual appropriately qualified clinician. No fabricated testimonials or ratings are included.

Google Fonts (Italiana and DM Sans) are loaded over the network; system fallbacks remain available.

## Visual generation
Original logo and photographs were created with the built-in image generator. Full image frames are retained; no CSS cover crops or image zoom animation is used. The composition adapts by moving hero and CTA text below images on narrow screens.

## Visual refinement
Visible frosted glass header, a light hero contrast gradient, personal-care portrait below the introduction label, full-width atelier photograph, one-line step headings and three-line copy, hover/focus image reveals, an emphasised enquiry form, and a full-width blended still-life footer with oversized brand lettering. Image frames are preserved.
