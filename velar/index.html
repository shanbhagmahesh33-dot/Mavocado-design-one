GOOD TO KNOW / 05

A Few Things.
Before You Go.

Clear answers. A smoother start.

What Do I Need To Rent A Car?

A valid driving licence and identity document. Age, licence tenure and overseas licence requirements depend on the vehicle and operator. These are confirmed before a live rental.

What Is Included In The Price?

The preview shows the daily rental, your selected options and the total for your dates. Fuel, ERP, parking, deposits and coverage terms are confirmed separately with the operator.

Can I Change My Pickup Location?

Yes, choose Gokarna, Kumta or Karwar in the search or booking panel. This concept assumes collection and return at the same location.

Can I Rent An Electric Or Hybrid Car?

Yes. Explore electric options such as the Porsche Taycan and BMW iX1, or choose a plug-in hybrid variant on eligible BMW and Mercedes-Benz models. The rate updates with your selection.

How Does This Booking Preview Work?

Configure a car and complete the booking preview. It is saved only on your device. This is a design concept: no vehicle is reserved, no payment is collected and no details are sent to a rental company.
