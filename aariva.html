02 / The Problem Worth Solving

People need care.
The website should offer clarity.

The design challenge was to make a complex set of hospital information feel manageable, especially for someone arriving with questions.

Too many places to begin

Specialties, doctors, check-ups and visit details need distinct routes without competing for attention.

Expertise that feels distant

A hospital can communicate capability while still sounding approachable to patients and their families.

Uncertainty beyond the appointment

Practical questions about preparation, insurance and follow-up deserve space alongside clinical discovery.

Design ObjectiveConnect reassurance, relevant information and a clear next action within one patient-first homepage.

03 / The Experience We Designed

One homepage.
Four meaningful moments.

A clear introduction leads into specialty discovery, doctor exploration and practical support for the visit.

A human introduction.

Welcome the whole family

A full-width family photograph makes the opening feel personal. Two navigation rows separate utility tasks from care discovery, with a direct appointment action.

Design note

Give the primary tasks a stable place. The quick-action row connects doctor search, check-ups and visit preparation without requiring a deep menu search.

Make expertise easier to explore.

Find a relevant specialty

Eight specialty cards pair plain-language descriptions with dimensional medical illustrations. A filled blue hover state gives each interactive card a clear response.

Design note

Keep the label, short explanation and action together. Repeated card proportions make the options easier to compare.

Put people behind the expertise.

Explore your care team

Search, specialty filters and consistent profile cards bring names, areas of care and appointment actions into a familiar browsing pattern.

Design note

Use one predictable card structure, with room for portraits and a clearly separated profile link and appointment control.

Support the visit, not just the booking.

Know what comes next

A visit companion groups preparation, insurance and follow-up information into three tabs. A visible selected state and numbered guidance help visitors stay oriented.

Design note

Separate each stage of the visit while keeping the guidance in one compact space. Familiar tabs let people revisit the information they need.

05 / Built for Consistency

A recognisable identity.
A consistent way to care.

A compact visual language keeps navigation, information cards and appointment actions connected.

Manrope, with room to breathe

A single sans-serif type family, clear size steps and considered line lengths keep the hospital’s information readable.

Blue for action. Navy for depth.

Cobalt blue identifies primary actions. Navy adds contrast, while warm white and pale blue give the content space.

Components with a shared language

Repeated cards, tabs, buttons and form controls keep the interface familiar as visitors move through the page.
