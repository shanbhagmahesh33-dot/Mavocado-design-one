Who We Are

Mavocado Design is a founder-led design and development studio led by Mahesh Shanbhag. This policy explains how we handle personal information through mavocado.design and related communications.

For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@mavocado.design.

Information You Share With Us

Depending on how you contact us, we may receive:

  • Your name, email address and telephone number.
  • Your company name, role and website or product link.
  • Project requirements, budget range, timeline and messages.
  • Information supplied when requesting a UX audit or resource.
  • Correspondence and documents shared during a business discussion.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment-card details, identity documents or confidential customer information through general website forms.

Information Collected When You Visit

Our hosting and analytics providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser, device information, requested pages, referring website and visit timestamps.

The website is currently hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Read the GitHub Privacy Statement.

How We Use Information

We use relevant information to:

  • Review and respond to enquiries.
  • Understand project requirements and prepare proposals.
  • Deliver agreed services and communicate about ongoing work.
  • Provide resources or updates you have requested.
  • Understand website usage and improve the experience, when analytics is enabled.
  • Prevent misuse, troubleshoot problems and maintain security.
  • Meet applicable legal, accounting and recordkeeping obligations.

Sending a project enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

Enquiry Forms And Service Providers

Our website uses FormSubmit to process enquiry forms and deliver submissions to the studio’s email address. Information entered into a form is transmitted to that provider when you submit it.

We also use hosting, email and other providers where needed to operate the website and communicate with you. Their handling of information is subject to their own terms, privacy notices and applicable agreements. Read FormSubmit’s privacy information.

Website Analytics

If you accept optional analytics, Google Analytics helps us understand page visits and interactions, including use of contact options and successful enquiry submissions.

Our custom analytics events are designed not to include the names, email addresses, telephone numbers or messages entered into enquiry forms. Analytics can involve cookies, identifiers and device or usage information.

You can change your choice using . Read our Cookie Policy for more information.

Browser Storage And Project Previews

The website stores your cookie choice locally. Some interface features and interactive project previews also use browser storage to support functionality.

Some demonstrations prepare files locally or store sample information in your browser. These are not necessarily connected to live business systems. Follow each preview’s notices and avoid entering sensitive information.

Marketing And Resource Requests

If you separately request updates or resources, we use the information needed to fulfil that request. You can ask us to stop marketing communications using an available unsubscribe option or by emailing hello@mavocado.design.

Sharing And International Processing

Information may be shared with providers supporting hosting, communications, analytics and agreed services. It may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to address fraud, security incidents or legal claims.

Providers may process information outside your country. Applicable protections depend on the provider, arrangement and relevant law. We do not sell personal information.

How Long We Keep Information

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including responding to enquiries, delivering services, maintaining relevant business records and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods vary by record type. Service providers and backups may have separate retention arrangements.

Your Choices And Requests

You can contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of information we hold about you, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a privacy concern.

We may need to verify your identity before acting. Some information may need to be retained for legal obligations or legal claims. Requests are handled according to applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information under our control. No website, email system or method of internet transmission can guarantee complete security.

Children’s Information

Our website and services are intended for business and professional audiences, not children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and address it.

Changes And Contact

We may update this policy when our website, services or practices change. The revision date identifies the latest version.

For questions or requests, email hello@mavocado.design with the subject “Privacy Request”.
