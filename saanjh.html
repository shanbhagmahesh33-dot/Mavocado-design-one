02 / The Design Challenge

Before the first bite.
Give people a reason to stay.

Bring the restaurant’s warmth into the browser while making practical dining decisions easy.

Let the atmosphere speak

Show the courtyard, team and shared-table moments in a consistent photographic language.

Make the menu worth exploring

Keep dish names, ingredients, prices and dietary indications clear, with useful ways to browse.

Connect browsing with a visit

Offer a clear table-planning route after visitors have explored the food and setting.

Design ObjectiveMake the experience feel like Saanjh: warm, considered and easy to return to.

03 / The Experience We Built

Feel the place.
Find your favourites.

Six connected moments, from a warm first impression to food discovery, people, gallery browsing and table planning.

A warm first impression

An evening begins on screen

A courtyard photograph fills the opening, paired with expressive serif typography, a custom sunset identity and one clear invitation to explore.

Design note

Use the restaurant’s own atmosphere to set the tone. Keep the navigation compact and the next action visible.

The menu, thoughtfully reimagined

Browse at your own pace

An interactive menu card opens into branded chapters for starters, mains and desserts. The same dish data powers the photographic menu and the booklet.

Design note

Category controls, page arrows, keyboard navigation and swipe gestures make the menu comfortable across devices.

A glimpse of the whole experience

Meet the place and its people

Ten consistent photographs connect the spaces, kitchen, team and guests. Category filters and a full-screen viewer make the gallery easy to explore.

Design note

Position the gallery immediately before the table planner, so the final invitation follows a fuller picture of the restaurant.

Food discovery, without the guesswork

Find your next favourite

Photographic dish cards bring ingredients, prices and dietary markers together. Category tabs and a vegetarian filter help visitors narrow the menu without losing context.

Design note

Keep useful details close to the photograph. Consistent card layouts make dishes easier to compare.

The people behind the welcome

Put a face to the hospitality

Named chef, service and housekeeping portraits introduce the people who make Saanjh feel like home. Consistent uniforms and warm courtyard settings connect the team with the rest of the site.

Design note

Give each portrait enough room. Keep heads and hands in frame, with names and roles positioned clearly below.

A clear invitation to come together

Make a little plan

The final invitation pairs warm copy with a focused table planner. Visitors choose a date, group size, time and seating preference, then save a plan on their own device.

Design note

Make the interaction honest and predictable: this preview saves a local plan and clearly explains that it does not send or confirm a reservation.

04 / Decisions Behind the Design

Expressive in feeling.
Practical in use.

The details support atmosphere and everyday browsing together.

One menu, two ways to browse

The photographic cards and menu booklet share dish information, keeping names, prices and ingredients consistent.

A coherent photographic world

Ochre plaster, ivory linen, brass tableware and green uniforms connect food, people and spaces.

Motion with a purpose

Subtle transitions mark chapter and photo changes. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

Room for the next step

A local table planner follows the gallery. It saves a visit plan without suggesting a confirmed reservation.

05 / The Saanjh Design Language

Warmth in the palette.
Character in the type.

A compact identity system gives every section the same unmistakable sense of place.

Petrona meets Onest

A characterful serif with warm italics pairs with a clear sans-serif for navigation, labels and longer reading.

Ivory, forest and vermilion

Warm neutrals give the photography room. Deep green grounds the interface; vermilion draws attention to key moments.

A shared set of details

Sunset emblems, fine dividers, dietary icons and directional arrows repeat across cards, menus and controls.
