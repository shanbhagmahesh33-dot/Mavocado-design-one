# Veyara Jewels

Open index.html to preview the storefront. No build step is required.

The assets folder contains three campaign banners, sixteen product images, six collection images, eight illustrative portraits and local fonts. Keep this folder next to index.html when uploading the site.

Interactions: hero carousel, category and material filters, price sorting, search, product detail dialog, local wishlist and bag, offer copy and email field.

Wishlist and bag are stored on the current browser only. Checkout, customer accounts, live stock, subscription delivery and appointments require backend integration. Product pricing, materials, ratings, offers and promises are sample design content to verify before launch.

Original Veyara brand concept and generated imagery. Responsive 20px gutters, sharp card/button edges, 12px collection/product gaps, reduced motion support.
