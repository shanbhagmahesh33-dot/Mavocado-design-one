02 / The Design Challenge

Make the water inviting.
Make the next step simple.

Help first-time and returning surfers picture the experience, compare rides and choose a suitable starting point.

Lower the first-wave barrier

An inviting hero and encouraging, concise content make the school approachable without overwhelming new surfers.

Explain different ways to ride

Shortboard, longboard and bodyboard discovery combines action photography with a hands-on 360-degree viewer.

Connect energy to a clear enquiry

Three lesson formats lead into session details and a focused form with the chosen session already selected.

03 / The Experience We Built

From the first feeling
to the first wave.

Six connected moments turn a visual world into an experience visitors can explore, compare and act on.

First impressions

Less fear. More feeling.

An ocean-wide surfing image, a glass header and a gently floating headline establish the Bluehour world.

An ocean classroom

Trade the ordinary for ocean.

Moving water, detailed fish and swaying seagrass create an immersive backdrop with an optional ocean soundscape.

Three ways to ride

Find your kind of ride.

Large action photographs introduce shortboarding, longboarding and bodyboarding with a direct route to exploration.

Interactive board discovery

A full turn. A better feel.

Drag or use the keyboard to rotate each board through 360 degrees, revealing the deck, rails, underside and fins.

Session discovery

Start small. Feel it all.

Three lesson cards use meaningful icons, clear inclusions and a filled middle CTA to support comparison.

Good people. Good water.

Come as you are.

A full-width surf lesson photograph and concise copy bring the people behind the experience into focus.

04 / Decisions Behind the Design

Motion with purpose.
Clarity at every step.

A distinctive underwater world is supported by readable layouts, practical controls and accessible ways to explore.

A clear frame for the hero

The translucent sticky navigation sits above the surfing image, keeping the rider framed within the hero.

A physical way to explore

The board viewer supports full rotation with pointer dragging, touch interaction and keyboard controls.

Sound that stays optional

An explicit audio toggle enables a soft surf wash that responds to scrolling. Audio begins only after a visitor activates it.

Room for different preferences

Reduced-motion settings, a pause control, labeled form fields and keyboard-friendly controls support the visual experience.

05 / The Design Language

Sharp type. Deep blue.
Built for the water.

Ocean blue, electric cyan and white define a sporty identity with angular headings, sharp controls and large visual moments.

An original surf identity

A wave mark and condensed Bluehour wordmark connect the school to the movement of water.

Sporty, readable typography

Russo One creates sharp, expressive headings. Barlow Condensed supports the wordmark and labels; Archivo carries the practical details.

One connected ocean world

Coordinated Indian surf imagery, a deep-water backdrop and photographic marine details hold the visual direction together.

06 / What the Project Delivered

One school.
A complete digital experience.

A responsive surf school landing page, original identity and interactive front-end, included with self-contained local assets for static hosting.

Identity & visual system

An original wave mark, sporty typography, consistent ocean colors and six coordinated portfolio shots.

Interactive board discovery

Three board shapes with 360-degree drag rotation, keyboard controls and visible decks, rails and fins.

Motion & ocean ambience

A floating headline, animated water, marine life, hover effects and an optional scroll-responsive surf soundscape.

A focused enquiry journey

Session details, preselected lesson choices and a downloadable enquiry summary. The form does not send messages or confirm bookings.
