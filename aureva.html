02 / The Design Challenge

Make exploration feel easy.

The design makes the offer easy to understand and gives visitors a clear next step.

Organise a broad offer

Give each service a clear place and make the options easy to compare.

Explain the visit

Use short, plain-language sections to show what the appointment involves.

Prepare an enquiry

Let visitors choose a service and assemble a request they can copy.

How the experience works.
